This is the raw independent read-only seven-image review. Review reports, inputs and source snapshots retain their original bytes. Its nested manifest describes the original ignored capture, including binaries that are deliberately omitted here; archive-map.json records every original file and its disposition. The surrounding archive manifest covers every retained file. ELF and binary identities alone cannot reproduce the original artifact review. Application and partition binaries are saved separately under firmware/development with LFS; ELF files remain in the ignored original build/review directories. No build or runtime test is performed by this packaging step.

Large build.ninja and compile_commands.json inputs are losslessly gzip encoded. archive-map.json records both their decoded original identities and stored identities. Decompression recovers the exact captured bytes.
