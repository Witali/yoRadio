## Reuse and numerical boundary

The new component extracts unchanged butterfly, bitreverse and backward bodies
from pinned Xiph libvorbis1.3.7. The generator checks their original source hash
and exact bytes; allocating init/clear and unused forward are excluded. Types
and the original decimal constants are specialized to binary64. Lookup tables
belong to the caller. The BSD license is retained.

Exact signed spectrum integers are rounded once as S*2^-512, nearest-even;
source.exponent+512 stays in int64 metadata. All36-limb signed shapes through
1151 magnitude bits have normal binary64 inputs, without a new source-exponent
cap or early clipping. The transform reuses one N+8 double array, passing its
+8 position as both input/output. The leading padding keeps upstream rotation
pointers inside their allocated array without changing transform arithmetic.

This is approximate binary64 IMDCT after the exact front end. Its normwise
bound does not establish universal relative accuracy near cancellation or a
final PCM error. Arbitrary finite floor0 gain spans need a separate route.
